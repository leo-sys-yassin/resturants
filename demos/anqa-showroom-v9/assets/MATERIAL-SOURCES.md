Walnut: https://polyhaven.com/a/walnut_veneer — CC0 diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness, 2K. Same library used on all furniture, header plates, logo backing and interior entrance trim.
Plaster: https://polyhaven.com/a/plastered_wall_04 — CC0 scanned color, normal and roughness, 2K. Diffuse normalized to a low-contrast neutral modulation of the requested warm beige palette; normal strength kept subtle.
Floor: generated fine-pored limestone texture retained from v8, tinted warm greige, reduced bump.
Facade: generated vein-cut travertine texture retained from the approved original facade/v8.
Street: generated opposite streetscape retained from v8; foreground pavement, road and curbs are geometry.
Lighting: diffuse-only Cycles irradiance, OIDN denoising, separate from material color. Ceiling geometry/light definitions retained exactly from v8. Furniture LED tint approximates a 3000K blackbody; no real-world lux/CRI measurement is claimed.
